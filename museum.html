Exhibit I — The First Final Meetup

One day the plane will land in Karachi.

The airport doors will slide open.

Crowds will move everywhere.

But in that entire crowd, there will be one face that matters.

Yours.

Not through a screen.
Not through a call.

Actually there.

After months of waiting, the distance finally disappears.

Exhibit II — Our Home

Someday we will step into our own home.

Not a temporary place.

Ours.

A gaming room.
A quiet prayer space.
A small corner where we sit for hours talking about everything.

Slowly every wall, every chair, every room will fill with our habits and our laughter.

Until the house stops feeling like a building…

and starts feeling like our world.

Exhibit III — Quiet Movie Night

We finally watch that movie we kept delaying.

Snacks everywhere.
Lights dim.

Halfway through we realize something important.

The movie is actually terrible.

But it doesn't matter.

Because the moment isn't about the movie.

It's about sitting there together and laughing about how bad it is.

Exhibit IV — Work Evenings

Some evenings will be simple.

You will be working quietly.

Coffee beside you.

I will pretend to focus on my own work but occasionally look up just to see you there.

Two people sitting in the same room.

Doing nothing special.

Yet somehow it feels perfect.

Exhibit V — Game Night

Controllers ready.

Competition begins.

You insist you're about to win.

I insist you absolutely are not.

The score probably won't matter.

But the arguments, the teasing, and the laughter definitely will.

Exhibit VI — Tough Nights

Some nights won't be great. (Niagara Falls of Hell NOOOOOOOO)

Blankets everywhere.

Chocolate and snack supplies mysteriously increasing.

You complaining dramatically while I sit beside you refusing to let you deal with it alone.

I cozily hug you and warm you up for hours, not leaving your side while feeding you the snacks.

It won't be fun.

But it will still somehow feel comforting.

Because we'll face it together.

Exhibit VII — The Kitchen

The kitchen will become the most chaotic room.

Ramen cooking.

Counters messy.

You showing up mostly to talk instead of actually cooking.

Sometimes we will try cooking together.

Sometimes it will go perfectly.

Sometimes we will almost burn the entire house down.

Either way, the chaos will be part of the story.

The Final Room ~ Our Wedding

This room, you just entered...

It means you're curious, you want to know about this, you want it to happen.

This IS our possible upcoming future, but, this one won't be written down.

We are going to live this one for ourselves, and we are going to enjoy it at that moment instead of spoiling it by talking about it this far back.
