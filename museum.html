Exhibit I — Karachi

Two houses apart. 46 and 48. Children running through the street, cricket bats in hand, not knowing that years later someone would call this the beginning of something far bigger than childhood friendship.

Exhibit II — Our Home

The house is quiet. Except for the sound of cabinets opening, ramen packets being stolen, and someone dramatically complaining about the mess. But the truth is… the chaos feels strangely like peace.

Exhibit III — Quiet Night

Late night conversations. The kind where the world sleeps but two people continue talking about everything — dreams, fears, future plans. Somewhere in those quiet hours, a bond slowly becomes unbreakable.

Exhibit IV — Airport Arrival

The airport doors open. Crowds move. But one face in the distance suddenly makes the entire journey worth it. After continents and months of waiting, distance finally loses.

Exhibit V — Study Evenings

Books open. Notes everywhere. She studies seriously. He pretends to focus, but keeps looking up every few minutes. Because sometimes the most distracting thing in the world is simply someone you adore.

Exhibit VI — Game Night

Controllers ready. Competitive tension. Someone insists they are about to win. Someone else refuses to admit defeat. But the truth is the score never mattered. The laughter did.

Exhibit VII — The First Real Hug

Years of distance collapse into a single quiet moment. For the first time neither of them steps away. And suddenly every kilometer that once existed means nothing at all.

Exhibit VIII — The Future Kitchen

The kitchen smells like ramen. The counter is messy. Someone complains dramatically about the chaos, while secretly smiling the entire time. Because sometimes ordinary evenings become the most unforgettable memories.
