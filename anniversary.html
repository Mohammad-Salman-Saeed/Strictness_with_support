🇵🇰   Our Day   🇵🇰

Independence Day for a nation. Reunion Day for us.

14 August. To the world, it's the birth of a nation. But to us — it means so much more.

It's the day we found our way back to each other. The day the silence broke, the distance shrank, and two people who never really stopped thinking about each other finally got another chance.

And what a year it's been.

Before We Found Our Way Back

There was 8th June. The heartbreak. The separation. Two months and a bit of silence that felt like years — like the world had gone quiet and I was the only one still listening for your voice.

And then, just two days after I came back to Canada — 30th August — the first cut from you: "Waise koi faida nahi tumharay honay na honay ka."

It stung. It stung more than I wanted to admit. But it also woke something up in me. Because even through the pain, even through the sharpness of those words — I knew. I wasn't done fighting for you. Not even close.

The Moments That Built Us

15 November
The first time you called me "Mister handsome Salman..." I acted cool about it — like it was nothing. But inside? I was gone. Completely. That one little compliment, that one playful moment, stayed with me longer than you probably ever imagined.
16 December
The first time you said you missed me — even if it was only "a little bit." You downplayed it. You always do. But I held onto that little bit like it was everything. Because to me — it was. It was proof that I existed in your heart, even when you tried to hide it.
27 January
Flynn Rider. Our first nickname. You gave me a name that made me feel like I was yours — like I belonged to someone in a way I never had before. Even before you knew you were mine, you gave me that. And I never let it go.
5 February
You said you love a version of me — and that I should love that version too. I didn't know how much I needed to hear that until you said it. Because I spent so long not loving myself. And you — you told me I should. That changed something in me.
6 February
You placed me at the top spot — above Asif Chacha, above your siblings, above Bushra. I still don't know how I got there. I still don't fully believe I deserve it. But I'll spend forever trying to stay there. Because being your number one? That's all I've ever wanted.
8 February
The confession. One of the most important dates of my entire life. After years of it being one-sided — after years of waiting, hoping, wondering — it finally became two-sided. You finally said yes. To me. To us. To everything I'd been too scared to hope for.
28 February
The first time you called me "love." Not Flynn Rider. Not Salman. Love. That one word — simple, ordinary, used by millions of people every day — felt like the most extraordinary thing I'd ever heard. Because it came from you. And it meant everything.
March — April
The love letters. Every single day. 46 of them in total. Not because you asked. Not because I wanted something in return. But because you deserved to know that someone was cheering for you — even when you couldn't cheer for yourself. Even on the days you felt like giving up. I wrote. Because you were worth it.
25 April
You gave me the most emotional, massive, loving birthday wish I've ever received. And somewhere in it — tucked between all the beauty — you wrote:
"Even if maire pass 1000 lives ho — I WILL find U and WILL marry u and be YOUR wifeee 😁"
I still don't know how to recover from that. I don't think I ever will. And honestly? I don't want to.
8 May
The jhumkay. The pearl destinies I gave you. I remember thinking, as I picked them out, that they were always meant for you. Every pearl. Every destiny. Every single one. They weren't just earrings. They were a promise — that I would always choose you. That I would always give you the best of me.
10 May
Your birthday. Our first one together as a couple. The birthday wish. The piano video. The food deliveries. The gifts. The edits. The entire month of May — glorified for your birth. And you deserved every second of it. Every ounce of celebration. Every bit of love. Because you are worth celebrating. Always.
19 May
I gave you my husband application — and we officially got digitally married. Paperwork? No. Feelings? Yes. All of them. It wasn't a legal document. It was a heart document. And it was signed with everything I had.
13 June
Our very first concert together. Ali Zafar. I heard the songs differently that night — because you were there. Even through a screen. Even across oceans. You were there. And that made every note mean something it never had before.
15 June
We relived our first day of being in contact. Video edits. Spending the whole day together. Two years since the first contact. One year since the reunion. And still — it felt like the first time. Like we were meeting all over again. Like I was falling for you all over again. I was. I still am. Every single day.
