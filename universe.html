In some universes…

There are timelines where fate almost happened. Where two souls stood dangerously close to something infinite… but missed it by seconds. And sometimes, seconds are enough to rewrite entire lifetimes.

Universe One

Two houses. 46 kokan and 48 kokan. Separated by mere walls… but never meant to be separated by destiny. In this universe, two three-year-olds play outside under the same fading sun. They laugh. They run. They fall. But they never become each other’s first friend. They grow up as background characters in each other’s lives. A wave from the balcony. A polite smile in passing. They never know that the person who lived one house away was the one their heart would someday search for in every crowd. And life moves on… quietly missing something it never realized it lost.

Universe Two

2016-2018 The street feels alive again. Cricket arguments. Evening breeze. The kind of chaos only childhood neighborhoods understand. They stand on the same street. On the same team. Laugh at the same jokes. But in this universe… his eyes don’t look for her first. Her presence doesn’t feel different. She becomes just another memory from growing up. And that subtle pull… that invisible thread that tied them even in silence… never tightens.

Universe Three

2019 arrives quietly… and leaves quietly too. A long silence grows between them. Days turn into months. Months into years. In this universe, he never admits she stayed on his mind. He never replays her laugh when he can’t sleep. He convinces himself it was just a phase. And slowly, the version of him who would have loved her fiercely learns how to live without ever trying.

Universe Four

July 19th, 2024. A conversation that feels heavier than it should. A warning disguised as concern. A request to step back. In this universe… he doesn’t cry that night. He doesn’t feel that crack in his chest. He doesn’t realize what she means to him. He lets logic win. He lets distance grow. And because he never breaks that night… he never understands that some people aren’t meant to be “optional.”

Universe Five

Airports. Goodbyes. Karachi fading behind airplane windows. America. Then Canada. Screens replace sidewalks. Notifications replace presence. In this universe… distance becomes too loud. Time zones become excuses. Overthinking becomes routine. They slowly start choosing convenience over courage. And love — beautiful, fragile, growing love — loses to geography.

Universe Six

In this one… He feels like he loves too much. She feels like she isn’t expressive enough. He overthinks small pauses. She overthinks small silences. Instead of reassurance… they choose doubt. Instead of patience… they choose pride. And something that could have lasted forever ends because two people who loved each other were too afraid of not being enough.

But not this universe.

And if every universe collapsed… if every timeline erased our memories… if 46 was never next to 48… I would still search through infinity until I found you again, Manahil. Because you are not an accident in my life. You are destiny that survived distance, silence, fear — and even fate itself.
