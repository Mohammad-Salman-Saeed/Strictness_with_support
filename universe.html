In some universes…

There are timelines where fate almost happened. Where two souls came dangerously close… but missed by inches. And inches are enough to change everything.

Universe One

Two houses. 46 and 48. One narrow street. One shared sky. Afternoons filled with dust, cricket bats, scraped knees, and the kind of laughter only three-year-olds understand. In this universe… they never become each other’s first friend. They grow up seeing each other from balconies. Nodding politely. Smiling occasionally. But never knowing that the person living one house away was meant to become their entire world.

Universe Two

2015. The street comes alive again. Society kids shouting. Gully cricket arguments. Evening breeze carrying familiar voices. They play on the same team. Stand a few feet apart. But in this universe… his heart never pauses when she laughs. He never searches for her in the crowd. She never becomes the one face that feels different. She’s just someone he knows. Not someone he feels.

Universe Three

2019 fades quietly. A gap forms. Long. Heavy. Silent. In this universe… he lets the silence stay. He never wonders why she still lives in his thoughts. He never admits that maybe this isn’t just a crush. And slowly… the version of him who could have loved her learns to live without ever trying.

Universe Four

July 19th, 2024. A conversation. A warning. A request to step away. In this universe… he doesn’t cry that night. He doesn’t feel that sharp ache in his chest. He tells himself it’s fine. Just friendship. Just timing. Just life. And because he never cries… he never realizes that losing her would break him.

Universe Five

Airports. Karachi. America. Toronto. Screens instead of sidewalks. Time zones instead of tea-time walks. In this universe… distance grows louder than love. Calls become shorter. Replies become slower. And eventually… they stop trying because being strong alone feels easier than missing someone every night.

Universe Six

In this one… He overthinks. She feels like she isn’t expressive enough. He feels like he loves too loudly. They both start doubting themselves instead of choosing each other. Pride replaces patience. Silence replaces reassurance. And love… that was growing beautifully… ends not because it wasn’t real — but because fear was stronger.

But not this universe.
